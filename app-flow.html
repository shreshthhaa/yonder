From "we should go here someday" to "we're going Saturday"

How one friend group saves places from reels, plans together, and actually goes. Screen IDs match scope-and-flow.md. The small italic line on each step follows the "Bandra Bunch" user story.

The main journey

Eight steps, in the order a group uses them. After "Did you go?", the place is crossed off and the group goes back to its bucket list.

1

Get in

  • A1Login with phone or Google
  • A2Taste swipe: right or left on activities
  • A3Home city · location while using

MON Riya swipes right on cafés, pottery and comedy; left on clubs.

2

Join a group

  • B1Home: all my groups
  • B2Create · share invite link or code
  • B2Invite from contacts (optional)
  • B3Join via link or code

MON "Bandra Bunch" link goes into WhatsApp; Kabir joins with the code.

3

Save from anywhere

  • C1Share pop-up over Instagram, YouTube, Pinterest
  • C2Paste a link or a typed list
AI is sure → places pre-ticked → Save Partly sure → "one of these 3?" in step 4 No idea → "Tell you later"

TUE 11 PM Riya shares a speakeasy reel; 4 bars saved in 5 seconds.

4

Confirm

  • C3Pop-up when the app opens: Yes / pick / search / later
  • —Guess the place from the cover photo

THU "Which place was the late-night food post?" Riya types 3 letters and picks.

5

Browse the bucket list

  • D1List by category, with filters
  • D2Map toggle in the same tab
  • D3Place details · edit only your own

WED Sana flips to Map: pins around Bandra, Santacruz, Worli.

6

Plan with the AI

  • E0Plan tab: all plans + "New plan"
  • E1Tap answers: who's coming, when, kind, budget, where, must-haves
  • E2Ideas from your list + new for you

FRI 4 PM Kabir unticks Aman (away), so veg-only no longer applies.

7

Vote on the top 3

  • E3Drag to rank 1-2-3 (3/2/1 points)
  • E3"Doesn't work for me" flag
  • E3Only people coming vote · deadline

FRI 7 PM Sana flags the bar with no mocktails. Ocho wins with 8 points.

8

Go, then cross it off

  • E4Plan card: time, cost each, directions, booking link
  • D4Calendar + reminders
  • E5"Did you go?" → crossed off + rating

SUN Riya taps Yes, rates it 4 stars. Ocho moves to "Done".

↺ Loop: crossed-off places move to "Done", group memory learns ("Sana liked the mocktails"), and the next plan starts from step 5.

Inside a group: how the 4 tabs connect

In the app the tabs read Chat · Plan · Bucket list · Calendar. Here they're arranged by what each tab hands to the next.

Orange arrows bring things into the group; teal arrows move them between tabs.
