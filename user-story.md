# User Story: "Bandra Bunch" plans a Saturday

_Session 13, 20 Nov 2026. One end-to-end walk-through of the product (Draft v3 scope). Screen IDs refer to `scope-and-flow.md`. People and the new-place suggestion are illustrative; the saved places come from the real test links in `eval-set-reels.md`._

## The cast
| Person | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Riya** (24) | Works in Lower Parel. The group's reel-sharer. Loves cafés, workshops, comedy. Not into clubs |
| **Aman** (25) | Vegetarian. Often travels for work |
| **Kabir** (23) | Always up for bars and live music |
| **Sana** (24) | Doesn't drink, so needs good mocktails |

**Group:** "Bandra Bunch", 4 college friends in Mumbai. Riya is also in "Office gang" and "Cousins".

**Their problem today:** reels pile up in the WhatsApp group, nobody can find them later, and every Saturday ends in a 2-hour debate before they go back to the same café.

---

## 1. Getting in (A1–A3, B1–B3)
- Riya downloads the app and logs in with her phone number.
- **Taste swipe:** 15 cards. She swipes right on cafés, pottery, stand-up comedy and sunset spots; left on clubs and treks.
- Sets city: Mumbai. Allows location "while using the app".
- Creates **"Bandra Bunch"** and copies the invite link into their existing WhatsApp group. Aman and Sana tap the link and join. Kabir is on a laptop, so he types the 6-letter **code**.
- Each of them does their own quick taste swipe on joining.

## 2. Saving while scrolling (C1, C2, G1)
- **Tuesday, 11 pm.** Riya is scrolling Instagram and finds a reel about Mumbai's hidden speakeasy bars.
- She taps **Share → our app**. A small pop-up opens **on top of Instagram**: *"Found 5 places: ☑ PCO, Lower Parel ☑ Slink & Bardot, Worli ☑ Ocho, Santacruz ☑ Charlee, Santacruz ☑ Harbour Bar, Colaba."*
- She unticks Harbour Bar (too pricey), selects **Bandra Bunch** and **Office gang**, taps **Save**, and is back to scrolling in about 5 seconds.
- In Bandra Bunch's **Chat** tab, a card appears: *"Riya shared 4 places · Bars & pubs."* Kabir replies "PCO has been on my list forever 🔥" and taps 👍. The 4 places are automatically in the **Bucket list**.
- **Wednesday.** Sana opens the app and pastes a note from her phone: *"cafés to try: Subko, Kala Ghoda Café, Candies."* The AI splits it into 3 places; she saves them.

## 3. Confirming what the AI wasn't sure about (C3)
- **Thursday.** Riya had also shared a "where to eat in Mumbai after 1 AM" photo post. The caption didn't name any place.
- When she opens the app, a pop-up asks: *"We couldn't tell which place this is. Which one was it?"* with the post's thumbnail. She types "Bad…", picks the right place from the suggestions, and it's saved. (She could also tap **"Tell you later"**.)

## 4. Browsing the bucket list (D1–D3)
- Sana opens **Bucket list**: 7 places under "Bars & pubs", "Cafés", "Bakeries & desserts".
- She flips the toggle to **Map** and sees pins clustered around Bandra, Santacruz and Worli.
- She taps Ocho: "Santacruz · about ₹3,000 for two · closed Mondays · added by Riya · Kabir is interested."

## 5. Starting a plan (E0, E1)
- **Friday, 4 pm.** Kabir opens the **Plan** tab and taps **"+ New plan"**. Tap-to-answer questions:
  - **Who's coming?** Unticks **Aman** (out of town this weekend)
  - **When?** This weekend → Saturday → Evening
  - **What kind?** Bars & pubs, Food
  - **Budget per person:** ₹1,000–2,000
  - **Where?** Middle for everyone
  - **Must-haves:** auto-filled from group memory for the people coming: *Sana: good non-alcoholic options*. Aman's "vegetarian" is **not** applied because he isn't coming.
  - **Ideas from:** Both (our list + new)
  - **Anything else?** "It's Sana's birthday week, somewhere with good music"
- Riya and Sana see the plan appear and add in the thread: "I can only make it after 8."

## 6. Recommendations (E2)
The AI replies in the plan's thread, visible to everyone:
- **From your bucket list**
  1. **Ocho, Santacruz:** fits your budget (about ₹1,500 each), open Saturdays, good for a celebration. *Riya added it; Kabir is interested.*
  2. **Slink & Bardot, Worli:** hidden bar, moody vibe, opens 6:30 pm. *Riya added it.*
- **New for you**
  3. **A rooftop bar in Khar with live music** *(illustrative)*: not on your list yet, fits your budget, strong mocktail menu. *New for the group.*
- Kabir asks: "anything closer to Bandra?" The AI adjusts and swaps one option.

## 7. Voting (E3)
- Riya, Kabir and Sana (the people coming) **drag the 3 options into order**. Aman can see the plan but doesn't vote.
- Sana flags Slink & Bardot **"Doesn't work for me"**: no mocktail menu listed.
- Result after the 3-hour deadline: **Ocho wins (8 points)**.

## 8. The plan card and the outing (E4, D4)
- **Final plan card** in the Plan tab, with a short note posted in Chat:
  *Ocho, Santacruz · Sat 8:30 pm · about ₹1,500 each · Riya, Kabir, Sana · [Directions] · [Book: link to their booking page]*
- Added to the group's **Calendar**. Reminders arrive Friday evening and Saturday at 6:30 pm.
- Kabir taps the booking link and reserves a table (outside our app).

## 9. Afterwards (E5)
- **Sunday, 11 am.** "Did you go to Ocho?" → Riya taps **Yes**, rates it 4★. Ocho gets **crossed off** the bucket list and moves to "Done".
- Group memory learns: *"Sana: liked the mocktails at Ocho."*

## 10. Bonus moment: near a saved place (🟡)
- **Wednesday after work.** Riya opens the app in Lower Parel. The home screen shows: **"You're near PCO (600 m), from Bandra Bunch's list."** She shares it in the group: "PCO next week?"

## What this story shows (for your write-up, in your own words)
| Problem from your experience | Where the story solves it |
|------------------------------|---------------------------|
| Reels get lost in chats | §2: one tap, filed automatically into the group's bucket list |
| Hard to find when needed | §4: list by category + map |
| Long back-and-forth discussion | §5–7: tap-to-answer questions → 3 options → ranked vote with a deadline |
| Budget and time make "exploring not worth it" | §5–6: the AI checks budget, timing, area and who's coming *for* the group |
| End up at the usual place | §6: "new for the group" options + places the group already wanted |
| Ideas never acted on | §9: "Did you go?" closes the loop; §10: nudges when nearby |
